        How it stays safe

        • Gemma decides, rules verify. The checker can raise triage, never lower it.
        • RED sign ⇒ instant referral, before any more model reasoning.
        • Evidence, not guesses. Every danger sign Gemma finds is quoted from the note; a negation-aware keyword net double-checks it.
        • Bad readings are re-measured, vague notes get a clarifying question.
        • Low confidence or loops ⇒ human handoff.
        • Model crash ⇒ rules-only mode; the visit still completes.
        • Voice in, voice out. Gemma 4 transcribes speech on-device; advice is read aloud in the family's language.
        • No network needed. Records queue locally and sync when signal returns, de-identified (names never leave the device).
        • Private by design. Hide names on screen; wipe all data in one click.

        Demo protocol for a hackathon, not validated medical advice.
